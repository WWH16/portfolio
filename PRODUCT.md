# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Companies, startups, and product teams looking for high-quality freelancing partners, custom software development collaborations, and creative direction.

## Product Purpose
Showcase Nova Silva's creative direction, visual craft, and front-end expertise to win premium freelance projects and bespoke software development contracts.

## Brand Personality
Modern Green, sophisticated, bespoke, and interactive.

## Anti-references
Generic template layouts, standard SaaS landing page clichés (e.g. blue/indigo grids, identical card blocks), and static, uninspired presentation.

## Design Principles
1. Custom over Generic: Every section must feel bespoke, intentional, and high-fidelity, avoiding standard layouts.
2. Seamless Interactivity: Motion, parallax, and transitions should enhance the content, making it feel alive.
3. Editorial Clarity: High typographic contrast and spacious layout to communicate design professionalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA accessibility compliance (color contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body copy).
- Support for prefers-reduced-motion media query (fading instead of scrolling/scaling animations).
- Pure semantic HTML for screen reader compatibility.
